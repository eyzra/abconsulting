Who's Behind the Work

done the work himself.

Dr. Barnabas Amu — Founder & Lead Consultant

Dr. Barnabas Amu is an economist, educator and leader with a passion for developing people and unlocking human potential. He serves as Executive Director of City College of Education and Abuja International Academy, where he has spent years building structures and the people inside them.

He founded A.B Consulting to bring that same discipline to individuals and organizations outside the classroom — through structured coaching, corporate training and the accountability systems he is best known for, including the Personal Accountability Program and the Community Accountability Program.

Through Becoming Better with Dr. B, he hosts a growing community built around one idea: that consistency, properly understood, is something that can be taught.
