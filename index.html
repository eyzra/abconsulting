Our Purpose

We develop people.

We develop people who develop people.

Everything we build, coach and facilitate is designed to outlast the engagement itself. The goal is never dependency. The goal is growth that keeps compounding after we are out of the room.

Our Method

We build systems.

We build systems that outlast motivation.

Motivation is circumstantial. Systems are structural. When you feel like showing up and when you do not should produce the same result. That is what we build.

Our Commitment

We show up.

We show up for your growth the way you need to show up for your goals.

Consistently. Without excuses. The standard we hold for ourselves is the same standard we bring into every engagement with every individual and every organization we work with.

Our Conviction

We believe in consistency.

Consistency is not a personality trait. It is a decision made in advance.

This single idea is the foundation of every program, every retreat and every coaching session we run. You do not become consistent by feeling like it. You become consistent by deciding to, and then building the structure that makes the decision irreversible.

Who's Behind the Work

Led by someone who has
done the work himself.

Barnabas Amu, PhD, Founder and Lead Consultant

Barnabas Amu, PhD is an economist, educator and leader with a deep commitment to developing people and unlocking human potential. He serves as Executive Director of City College of Education and Abuja International Academy, where he has spent years building institutions and the people inside them.

He founded A.B Consulting to bring that same discipline to individuals and organizations outside the classroom, through structured coaching, corporate training and the accountability systems he is best known for. Known to his community as Dr. B, he believes that the gap between who you are and who you are becoming is closed one daily decision at a time.

Through Becoming Better with Dr. B, he hosts a growing community built around one idea: consistency, properly understood, is not a personality trait. It is a decision made in advance.
